# Prompt de imagem: Ynana no camarim do Alto Salão (papel pardo)

> Para usar num gerador de imagem (o Claude Code não gera imagens). Baseado na cena de abertura da versão antiga e no retrato `referencias/imagens/ynana-retrato.webp`.
> Envie o retrato junto como referência de personagem, se o gerador aceitar.

## Prompt (inglês, funciona melhor na maioria dos geradores)

Illustration drawn on brown kraft paper: visible paper fiber texture, warm tan background left partly bare, ink linework with sepia and black washes, white gouache highlights, selective accents of deep red only. Hand-drawn, slightly rough edges, like a page from a merchant's leather journal.

Scene: a ceremonial dressing room behind a grand hall at night. A young fox-hybrid woman stands before a tall ornate mirror, fastening a necklace at her throat with both hands, looking at her reflection with a calm, determined expression. Through a tall arched window behind her, the dark coastline and harbor lights. Beyond a half-open door, the warm glow of a crowded hall under a glass dome. On a side table: a tea tray and a worn leather notebook with salt-stained edges and a loose clasp.

Character (keep consistent): black fox ears with red inner fur and small white tufts; long, wavy, very dark brown hair, almost black, with wispy bangs; red eyes; light skin; black choker; thin gold chain with a round red gemstone pendant set in gold (the necklace she is fastening); elegant Victorian gothic black gown with a sheen, off-the-shoulder ruffles, laced bodice, satin roses in red and black on the skirt.

Mood: quiet before a public revelation, restrained emotion, candlelight. No text, no watermark, no chibi, adult proportions.
